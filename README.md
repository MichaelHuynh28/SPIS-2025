# AI Image Detector

> *For safety and peace of mind, allowing technological innovation to grow, and photography to keep its brilliance and organic beauty.*

---

## The Problem
When looking at the arts, we see lots of hostility towards the rapid evolution and mainstream spread of generative AI. As these models continue to evolve, there appears to be a growing concern about the potential replacement of photography and art as a direct result of the aforementioned systems. Additionally, on a day-to-day basis, it can be scary to navigate the internet and social media and be unsure as to whether or not what you see was created by humans or artificially generated.

---

## The Solution
One way to ensure that the spaces of art and social media are safer to navigate is by being able to distinguish the two types of images apart, thus the need to create an AI Image Detector was born. With technology like this, traditional and human made art pieces can shine brighter, and people can feel safer with more accountability as detection software can be implemented either on personal devices or on the online sites themselves. 

---

## Key Features
* **Efficiently classifies images:** Quickly determines whether an image is AI-generated or real.
* **Robust Data Augmentation Pipeline:** Trains a resilient model using random rotations, color adjustments, and transformations to prevent overfitting.
* **Live evaluation interface:** Allows for the uploading of new images and outputs a confidence score along with the binary result.

---

As our first large-scale programming project and our first collaborative project in this space, we faced technical problems from a lack of experience and knowledge as incoming freshmen working on a serious project. Using new tools like PyTorch and learning new concepts, such as understanding what a CNN is, pushed us out of our comfort zone. Another often overlooked challenge is that of collaboration—building chemistry and understanding between partners takes time in any setting, and is something we worked on throughout the development of this project. 

---

## Our Wins
* Designed a multilayered CNN with Batch Normalization and Global Average Pooling without bloating the model's size.
* Implemented several overfitting prevention tools, including heavy data augmentation and custom noise injection.
* Successfully built an inference script allowing users to upload new images and receive a confidence score breakdown alongside the final verdict.
